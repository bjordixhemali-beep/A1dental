Frequently asked questions.

Why does it cost less than in Italy?

The cost of living in Albania is lower, so prices are more affordable — with no compromise on quality. We use certified materials and modern technology.

How many days do I need to stay in Tirana?

It depends on the treatment. For check-ups and simple care one visit is enough. Implants usually need two visits of a few days each — we give you a clear plan from the start.

How do I reach the clinic?

Tirana has direct flights every day from major Italian airports. The clinic is in central Tirana, easy to reach.

Do you speak Italian?

Yes. We communicate fluently in Albanian, Italian and English, so you feel comfortable at every stage of treatment.

How do I get a quote?

Contact us on WhatsApp or fill in the form below. If you have an X-ray, send it to us — the initial assessment is free and with no obligation.

Is it safe to get dental work in Tirana?

Yes. We are a QKB-licensed clinic, we use certified materials and modern protocols. Every year many Italian patients choose Tirana for the quality and personalised care.

Do you offer a warranty on treatments?

Yes. Our treatments and materials come with a warranty; we explain the exact terms during your treatment plan. We also follow up after you return home for any check-up.

Do you help with travel and accommodation?

We give you all the information to organise your trip and stay in Tirana and advise you based on your needs, to make the visit as comfortable as possible.
